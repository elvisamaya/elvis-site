Elvis Amaya

I'm a third-year EECS student at Stanford interested in digital hardware, computer architecture, and AI accelerators. I like carrying a design from RTL all the way down to a physical chip layout.

I'm also drawn to where hardware meets the physical world (embedded firmware, real-time systems and sensing) and to making STEM more accessible for students from low-income communities, something personal to me as a first-generation college student.

Recently I built a systolic-array AI accelerator taken to a SkyWater 130nm layout, and a pipelined RISC-V processor.
